# Action sound effects

All action effects below are released under CC0 on OpenGameArt. Attribution is optional; source details are retained here for review.

- Both gunshot contexts use `../packs/sfx/sfx-gun-player.wav`, “snd_gunshot1.wav” by Spring Spring, at different volumes — <https://opengameart.org/content/various-sound-effects-0>
- Reload sounds: `../packs/sfx/sfx-reload-player.wav` and `../packs/sfx/sfx-reload-opponent.wav`, “singlebullet1.wav” and “clipload2.wav” by BMacZero — <https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sound-effects>
- Guard movement sounds: `../packs/sfx/sfx-guard-player.wav` and `../packs/sfx/sfx-guard-opponent.wav`, “thud3.wav” and “thud2.wav” by BMacZero — <https://opengameart.org/content/metal-impact-sounds>
- Bullet-on-defense sounds: `../packs/sfx/sfx-defend-player.wav` and `../packs/sfx/sfx-defend-opponent.wav`, “clink1.wav” and “bing1.wav” by BMacZero — <https://opengameart.org/content/metal-impact-sounds>
